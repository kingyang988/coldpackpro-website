2. Custom Sizes and Shapes

The right ice pack size depends on how it will be used. A small gel ice pack may be suitable for lunch bags, insulated shopping bags and compact retail packaging. Larger packs may be more suitable for insulated shipping boxes, food delivery containers or temperature-controlled transportation.

We evaluate custom dimensions according to your packaging layout. Common customization considerations include:

Pouch Length & Width: Exact millimeter/inch dimensions matched to your carton footprint.
Filled Pouch Thickness: Controlled profile so packs stack or fit around products smoothly.
Pouch Format: Flat pillow pouch or other suitable pouch configurations.
Internal Placement: Top, bottom, or sidewall alignment inside insulated boxes.
Packs Per Shipper: Optimizing unit size and pack count per box to minimize empty void space.

Rather than choosing a size based only on appearance, we recommend considering the available space, surrounding products and required cooling coverage. If you already have an insulated box or product package, share its internal dimensions with us so we can evaluate a suitable gel pack size for your project.

3. Custom Fill Weight and Capacity

Fill weight is another important specification when developing a custom gel ice pack. Different applications require different amounts of cooling material: a compact pouch may require a lower fill weight, while a larger insulated shipper may need a higher fill weight or multiple packs.

Why Does Fill Weight Matter?

Fill weight affects the amount of cooling material inside the pouch, as well as its final dimensions, shipping weight and handling characteristics. However, a heavier gel ice pack does not automatically guarantee a longer cooling time. Cooling performance also depends on the gel formulation, initial freezing conditions, box insulation, surrounding ambient temperature, pack arrangement and transportation environment. For this reason, we always recommend evaluating fill weight together with the intended application.

4. Custom Gel Formulation for Different Applications

The gel formulation is one of the most critical factors when developing a customized gel ice pack. Different applications require different cooling characteristics and temperature stability windows. Depending on your requirements, our production team evaluates suitable cooling-gel options across key parameters:

  • •Intended commercial or logistics application
  • •Target temperature range (chilled vs. sub-zero)
  • •Customer pre-freezing conditions
  • •Required cooling hold duration
  • •Single-use vs. reusable cycle durability
  • •Compatibility with the selected pouch film material
  • •Relevant destination safety and regulatory documentation

For example, gel ice packs used in food delivery have very different requirements from those used in insulated pharmaceutical packaging. Before confirming a formulation, it is important to clarify actual operating conditions and verify performance through testing.

Share Your Target Temperature Range Early: Please include your target temperature range and application when requesting a quotation so our engineering team can assess a suitable gel formulation rather than quoting a generic standard fill.

5. Custom Pouch Materials and Packaging Structure

The outer pouch protects the cooling gel and maintains product integrity during storage, freezing, transportation and end-user handling. Depending on your project, packaging film materials and multi-layer structures are evaluated according to:

Flexibility & Softness

Conforms smoothly around irregular containers, meal trays, or cooler walls.

Sealing Performance

Strong heat-seal seams designed to withstand pressure during transit and freezing expansion.

Puncture & Abrasion Resistance

Protects against sharp frozen corners, carton edges, and rough parcel sorting.

Low-Temperature Performance

Resists film brittleness and cracking when pulled directly from deep freezers.

Formula & Ink Compatibility

Ensures inner layers match the gel chemistry while outer layers hold crisp brand print.

Reuse & Handling Durability

Selected and validated via real drop and freeze-thaw tests prior to mass production.

Frequently Asked Questions (OEM & Private Label Gel Packs)

Can any color, size, fill weight, and gel formulation be combined without an MOQ?

No. Printed roll film, printing plate/cylinder setup, pouch-forming specifications, filling equipment runs, and custom gel formulations all influence MOQ and unit pricing. Share your target specifications at the inquiry stage so our factory team can confirm feasibility and exact MOQ.

Does a heavier gel ice pack fill weight automatically guarantee longer cooling time?

No. Fill weight determines total cooling capacity and pouch size, but real cooling duration also depends on gel formulation, initial freezing conditions, insulation quality, ambient temperature, pack placement, and shipping conditions.

How do I choose the right gel ice pack size if I already have an insulated shipping box?

Share the internal dimensions of your insulated box or bag along with product size. Our team can evaluate pouch length, width, filled thickness, and recommended quantity per box to maximize thermal coverage without wasted space.
